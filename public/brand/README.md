# Lux Commercial Capital — logo assets

## What's here

**/svg** — vector, scales to any size. Send these to anyone doing print, merch, signage or a website.
- `lockup-horizontal*.svg` — primary. Full colour, reversed (dark backgrounds), black, white.
- `lockup-stacked*.svg` — for square and narrow spaces.
- `mark-*.svg` — keystone icon alone. Favicon, app icon, watermark.
- `seal-lc-*.svg` — LC monogram roundel. Secondary mark.

**/png** — raster at 4x, transparent-free (baked backgrounds). Fine for slides, social and email. Not for print.

**/seal** — the LC Seal as its own kit.
- `svg/seal-lockup-horizontal*.svg` and `svg/seal-lockup-stacked*.svg` — full colour, reversed, black.
- `svg/seal-icon-*.svg` — roundel alone. Full colour, reversed, black, white, solid purple.
- `png/` — 4x raster of the stacked, horizontal, reversed lockups and the purple avatar tile.
- The Seal reads "LUX COMMERCIAL / CAPITAL ADVISORY". Use it for advisory-side material, document seals, wax-stamp style merch and favicons; use the Keystone as the primary corporate mark.

## Type

- Wordmark and headlines: Cormorant Garamond
- Body, labels, UI: Jost
- Both are free on Google Fonts.

## Rules

- Clear space: leave the width of the inner keystone on all four sides.
- Minimum size: horizontal lockup 120px / 32mm wide; icon alone 24px / 8mm tall.
- Keep the keystone gold on dark grounds. Drop it only in the one-colour files.
- Don't stretch, rotate, outline, or fill the arch solid. It is a line mark.
- Don't reset the wordmark in another typeface.
- On photography, use the reversed lockup.

## Before this goes to print or merch

Two things a designer should do once, in Illustrator or Affinity:

1. **Convert the wordmark type to outlines.** The SVGs here reference Cormorant Garamond as a live font. That's fine in a browser, but a printer or embroiderer without the font will get a substitute. Outlining makes the file self-contained.
2. **Add CMYK and Pantone builds.** The hexes above are RGB. Ask for a Pantone match on the aubergine and the gold before any physical production — gold especially shifts on uncoated stock.

## Handing off to marketers

Send the whole `brand` folder. Tell them:
- Vector (`/svg`) for anything that gets resized. PNG only for fixed-size digital placements.
- Social avatars: `png/avatar-purple.png` or `svg/mark-reversed.svg` on #2B1B3D.
- Website favicon: `svg/mark-fullcolour.svg`.
- Ads on photography or video: reversed lockup, never the full-colour one.
